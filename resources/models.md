# Models and bounded peer selection

The selected Debate method owns orchestration, voting, recovery, and reporting;
[shared usage](usage.md#review) owns its controls and defaults. This policy owns
model selection and disclosure. Use the current model by default; omit overrides
unless the user or higher-priority host instructions explicitly authorize different
models. Any authorized host-available model remains inside platform, project,
provider/data, tool, scope, and resource permissions.

Distinct contexts and independent approaches matter; model names, version, price,
effort, and throughput do not establish intelligence or independent errors.
Same-model peers are permitted. Authorized model diversity is not proof of
independence or superior quality. Do not require a ladder or claim XL status.

Check actual host model/settings support within approved resource limits. Broader
provider disclosure, tools, scope, or budget require approval; do not route around
restrictions with credentials or provider CLIs. Report actual selections/settings
and substitutions, with measured/estimated/unavailable telemetry distinguished from
quality acceptance. Explicit review delegation does not authorize application or
research model calls. Invocation does not prove savings, independence, or quality.
