# Reference models and bounded review selection

Any host-available model is eligible within platform, project, data/provider,
tool, scope and resource permissions. Names below are dated references, not a
whitelist. Prefer economy-capable initial reviewers, stronger middle-stage
reconciliation, and strongest/XL-capability synthesis. Prefer 5.5+ where suitable;
older/unlisted models are not prohibited. Tiers are operational preferences, not
parameter counts or guaranteed intelligence from names, price, effort or throughput.

| Role | Agents | Historical reference | Previously usable alternative |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Independent review | 5 | `gpt-6-luna` | `gpt-6-luna` |
| Reconciliation | 3 | `gpt-6.1-terra` | `gpt-6.1-sol` |
| Final synthesis | 1 | `gpt-6-astra` | `gpt-6-astra` |

Reference date: 2026-11-04. Prior use establishes successful dispatch, not measured
quality, cost superiority, physical size or availability in another host. Check
actual tools, model/settings support, context for sources plus reports/output,
evidence access and budget. Unknown tier fit does not prohibit use. If distinct
tiers are unavailable, same-model distinct agents or strongest-available profiles
may run; disclose an unmet/unverified ladder without claiming XL qualification.

Preauthorized substitutions inside existing boundaries require no repetitive
model-ID approval. Broader provider disclosure, tools, scope, budget or method/stage
counts require approval. Preserve five independent reviewers, three independent
whole-set reconcilers and one mandatory synthesizer even after unanimous clean
reports. Distinct agents, not nine different models, establish the procedural roles.
No silent single-agent fallback may claim full hierarchy completion.

Use at most one additional invocation per affected role, shared across retry,
fallback and escalation, inside the approved total call/token/time/spend caps.
Recover from omissions, truncation or tool/evidence failure when useful; disputes
may remain unresolved if evidence is missing. Votes/self-confidence are not
verification. Preserve attempts and IDs; affected downstream rechecks consume the
total budget. Persistent required-role failure or exhausted budget leaves incomplete
work explicit. Models cannot supply missing source facts.

Report eligibility, evidence-stage completion, preferred-tier conformance and
quality acceptance separately, with actual selections/settings, substitutions,
coverage and measured/estimated/unavailable telemetry. Invocation alone is not a
quality or savings benchmark. Only explicitly selected review delegation is
authorized; it does not authorize application/research model calls.
